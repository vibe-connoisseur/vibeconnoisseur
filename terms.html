Terms and community rules

Where's The Vibe / Updated 11 November 2026

These terms apply to the Where's The Vibe website and app, run by The Vibe Connoisseur. By creating an account, following profiles or posting, you agree to them.

Zero tolerance

There is no place on Where's The Vibe for objectionable content or abusive users. You must not post or send anything that is:

  • hateful, discriminatory, harassing, bullying or threatening
  • sexually explicit or nude
  • violent, or promotes self-harm or dangerous activity
  • illegal, including selling drugs, weapons or counterfeit tickets
  • spam, scams, fake events or misleading ticket links
  • someone else's content without permission, or an impersonation of another person or brand

Content that breaks these rules is removed, and the account behind it can be suspended or deleted without warning.

Brand and DJ profiles

Brand and DJ profiles are checked before they go public. You must be, or be allowed to act for, the brand or DJ you set up. Everything you post is public. You keep ownership of it, and give us permission to show it in the website and app.

Events

Events are listed for information. Tickets are sold by the organiser or their ticket site, which is responsible for the event, refunds and entry. Check age limits and details with the organiser before you go.

Reporting and blocking

Every post and profile has a Report option, and you can block any profile so you no longer see it. We review reports within 24 hours and remove content that breaks these rules.

Your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. See the privacy policy for what we store.
