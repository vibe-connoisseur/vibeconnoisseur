Privacy policy

Where's The Vibe / Updated 20 October 2026

Where's The Vibe is run by The Vibe Connoisseur, a London events platform. This page explains what the website and the Where's The Vibe app collect, and what they don't. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Your location

If you tap Near me, the app asks for your location to sort events by distance and show you on the map. Your location is used on your phone only. It is never sent to us or stored anywhere. You can switch it off any time in your phone's Settings.

Saved events and reminders

Events you save, and the reminders for them, are kept on your phone only. Deleting the app removes them.

Newsletter

If you sign up for the weekly newsletter, we store your email address so we can send it. It is kept in a private spreadsheet and used only to send the newsletter. We don't sell or share it. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or email us and we'll remove you.

Event listings and payments

Organisers who pay for a Vibe Approved listing do so through Stripe on our website. Stripe handles the card details; we only receive the event name, date, contact email and Instagram handle given at checkout.

Services we use

The map is drawn with tiles from CARTO and OpenStreetMap, the site is hosted by Netlify, and fonts load from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services see your IP address when they deliver their content. Ticket links open the organiser's own ticket site, which has its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we'll reply within 30 days.
